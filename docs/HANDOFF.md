# Staff handoff guide (15–30 minutes)

Give this to the clinic receptionist / admin after go-live.

## Daily use

1. Open **Staff login** (footer link) or `/admin/login`
2. Sign in with the email/password you were given — **change password on first week**
3. **Appointments** tab shows upcoming requests
4. For each new booking:
   - **Confirm** when the slot is OK
   - **Cancel** if the patient called to cancel or you cannot accommodate
   - **Complete** after the visit (keeps the list clean)

## Filters

Use Pending / Confirmed to focus on today’s work.

## Services & hours

- **Services:** update name, duration, guide price, or hide a service (uncheck Active)
- **Hours:** set open times per weekday; mark Sunday (or any day) closed
- Changes apply to **new** bookings immediately

## What patients see

- They book at your website → **Book now**
- They get an email when the request is submitted (if email is configured)
- Status starts as **Pending** until you confirm

## If something goes wrong

- Double-booking blocked automatically — if a slot disappears, it was taken
- Patient needs a different time → cancel the old request and ask them to rebook (or book for them with their details)
- Contact your website provider for domain/email/hosting issues
