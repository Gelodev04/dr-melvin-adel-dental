# Packaging & pricing (Philippines)

Starting suggestions — adjust after your first 2–3 clients. Quote in **PHP**.

## Setup (one-time)

**Includes:** branded marketing site, online booking, staff admin, domain connect, seed content, 30-min training.

| Package | Suggested range | Notes |
|---------|-----------------|-------|
| Starter | ₱25,000 – ₱45,000 | Template rebrand, up to ~8 services, 3 dentists |
| Standard | ₱45,000 – ₱75,000 | Extra pages (FAQ, promo), custom photos, copy polish |
| Premium | ₱75,000+ | Multi-doctor calendars polish, custom sections, launch ads help |

## Monthly retainer

**Includes:** hosting, SSL, uptime monitoring, appointment emails, minor content edits (e.g. hours, prices).

| Tier | Suggested | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Host + support | ₱2,500 – ₱5,000 / mo | Best default offer |
| Growth | ₱5,000 – ₱8,000 / mo | Includes monthly SMS reminder allowance (when enabled) |

## Add-ons (upsell after MVP)

| Add-on | Ballpark |
|--------|----------|
| SMS reminders (Semaphore) | Setup ₱5,000 + per-SMS pass-through |
| Deposit via PayMongo / GCash | Setup ₱8,000 – ₱15,000 |
| Facebook Messenger booking link/page | ₱5,000 – ₱12,000 |
| Extra landing / promo page | ₱3,000 – ₱8,000 |
| Tagalog UI pass | ₱8,000 – ₱15,000 |

## What is / isn’t included

**Included in MVP package:** public site, booking with conflict checks, admin appointments/services/hours, email notify.

**Not included (sell later):** patient medical records, HMO claims, inventory, multi-branch, full EMR.

## Sales tip

Lead with the **live Dr. Melvin H. Adel Dental Clinic demo**. Close on time saved for the front desk and fewer Facebook “available ba?” threads — then quote setup + retainer as one clear number.
